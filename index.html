Catalyst 1st Strategy

1. Catalyst: Find a credible dated event that could reprice the stock.

2. Price and volume: Look for accumulation, reversals or constructive pullbacks.

3. Fundamentals: Verify earnings, guidance, financial strength and dilution risk.

4. Entry: Prefer early setups with a clear invalidation point, not an extended chase.

5. Decision: Watch, Buy, Hold or Reject based on evidence.

6. Review: Journal outcomes and improve the method.
